# PulseQ Sample Data & Production Readiness Checklist

This document details all sample, dummy, and placeholder data currently used in the PulseQ demonstration application, identifying the exact files and data structures that must be populated with verified production content prior to public launch.

---

## 1. Doctors & Medical Credentials
- **File**: `pulseq/src/app/data/doctors.data.ts`
- [ ] **Doctor Profiles & Names**: 15 sample doctors (`Dr. Ayesha Rahman`, `Dr. Hamza Qureshi`, etc.) currently have mock IDs (1–11, 101–104).
- [ ] **PMDC / PMC Registration Numbers**: Verify license numbers for all practicing doctors against Pakistan Medical & Dental Council.
- [ ] **Degrees & Fellowships**: Current degrees (`MBBS`, `FCPS`, `MRCP`, `FRCS`) are generated by sample rotation `DEGS[id % DEGS.length]`. Replace with verified medical qualifications.
- [ ] **Doctor Portraits**: Sample photos currently use UI avatars and colored gradients. Replace with high-resolution professional headshots.
- [ ] **Consultation Timings & Days**: Actual clinic operational schedule, days of week, and OPD shift timings must be synchronized with hospital management systems.
