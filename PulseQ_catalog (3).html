Questions

The fine print, in big print.

Still stuck? Contact our team and a real person will reply.

Do I pay online?

You choose. Pay online when you book, or pay at the clinic when you arrive, depending on what the doctor offers.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

Yes. Cancel from your appointments list, or reschedule within the time limit the clinic sets. You see the limit before you confirm.

Does the AI give medical advice?

No. Pulse helps you find the right kind of doctor and book a time. It does not diagnose. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Can I talk to Pulse in Urdu?

Yes. Speak or type in Urdu, English or a mix of both.

How are doctors verified?

Every doctor and facility is checked before their profile goes live on PulseQ.

Know your signs

Two quick checks that can save a life.

Heart disease is the world’s leading cause of death, and breast cancer is the most common cancer in women. Both are far easier to treat when caught early.

Heart attack checkWarning signs and your risk2 min →
Warning signs
  • Pressure, squeezing or heaviness in the chest
  • Pain spreading to the left arm, jaw, neck or back
  • Shortness of breath, cold sweat, nausea or dizziness
  • In women, signs can be milder: unusual tiredness, back or jaw pain, indigestion
Do not wait and do not drive yourself. Call your emergency number now (Rescue 1122 or Edhi 115) and sit down and stay calm.
Breast health checkSelf-exam guide and warning signs2 min →
Once a month, a few days after your period ends
  • Look in a mirror for changes in size, shape or skin
  • Feel each breast and armpit with flat fingers, in small circles
  • Warning signs: a new lump, dimpling, nipple turning inward, discharge, redness or swelling
Most lumps are not cancer, but see a doctor within 2 weeks of finding any change. Women 40 and over should ask about a mammogram.

Lifemap gives general guidance based on common screening and vaccination advice. It is not a diagnosis. Your doctor may change the timing for your health, family history or local schedule. In an emergency call 1122 or 115.
